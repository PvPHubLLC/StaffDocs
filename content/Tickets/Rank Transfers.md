---
title: 5. Rank Transfers
---
> ℹ️  Handled by [[Support]] and above until escalation is necessary.

If you find a [[Tickets/general]] ticket is a rank transfer, please ask for the **Tebex Transaction ID** (for proof of purchase).

> **Example Tebex ID:** `tbx-XXXXXXXXXXXXXX-XXXXXX`

Once obtained, please hand over the ticket to an [[Administrator]] or above - This should be done **concisely** with **clear communication and intent**.

`Example:`
```
User: I'd like to transfer my <rank> from <username> to <new-username>.

Support: We can do that, can you please provide the Tebex ID? It should look like
tbx-XXXXXXXXXXXXXX-XXXXXX

User: tbx-XXXXXXXXXXXXXX-XXXXXX

Support: Awesome, I'll hand this over to @Administrator now! Please be patient.

User: Thanks

*Continued by admin*
```

<details>
<summary><b>Administrator and above</b></summary>

Once handed over to a higher authority, admins should ensure the transaction exists
through searching in the payment channel.

If the payment is confirmed, a note should be made of the operation in the **Action Log** channel, with the <b>Tebex ID</b>, and usernames.

<code>
tbx-XXXXXXXXXXXXXX-XXXXXX <br/>
From: OriginalUsername -> NewUsername
</code>

The rank should now be transferred in game.

</details>
