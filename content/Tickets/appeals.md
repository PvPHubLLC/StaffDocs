---
title: 1. Appeal Tickets
---
> ℹ️ [[Moderator]] and above may handle this ticket type.

When a player has been punished, either muted or banned, they may appeal their punishment.

### Mutes
If a person has been muted and the evidence is valid, the person will stay muted. If they are being remorseful from the beginning and it's their first mute, you may unmute this person.

In the case where the proof isn't valid, simply unmute the person, and apologise for the inconvenience.

### Bans
There are two types of bans. Anticheat bans and staff bans. 
#### Anticheat
In the case of an AntiCheat punishment, look up the player’s logs by typing their username in the Discord search bar and reviewing their alerts. Based on the alerts send, and the AntiCheat guides [[AntiCheat/Polar]] and [[AntiCheat/TotemGuard/]], you make a decision whether the ban is valid or not. Keep in mind some checks may false, while others never. 


> If you are unsure, don't hesitate to ask the opinion of other staff members. If that still doesn't draw a conclusion, please notify a [[Developer]], so they can take a closer look at the situation.
#### Staff
When a player has been banned by a staff member simply lookup his username in  [🧵・punishment-evidence](https://discord.com/channels/927617818525052988/986386885557493760), to validate the evidence posted.
