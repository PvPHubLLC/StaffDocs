```
# :tada: Private Events Guide

**How to Host:**
1. Use `/private-events`
2. Talk to the **"Setup Event" NPC**
3. Talk to the **"Start Event" NPC** - choose **Participate** or **Spectate**
4. Use `/open-event` to let others join
5. Use `/advertise-event` to broadcast an invite to the entire server
6. Use `/invite` to invite specific players
7. Use `/start` when you have enough players

**Host Controls:**
• `/controls` - Manage event border
• `/hostmode` - Toggle host abilities while spectating
• `/respawn {user}` - Respawn a player
• `/mute` - Mute a player
• `/kick` - Remove a player

**Monthly Events & Player Caps:**
• **Silver** - 1 event | 30 player cap
• **Gold** - 2 events | 30 player cap
• **Diamond** - 7 events | 40 player cap
• **Ruby** - 15 events | 60 player cap

*Higher ranks include all lower rank perks!*

**:warning: Important Note:** This is a private event - you decide whether someone gets kicked or not! You make the rules for your own event.
```
